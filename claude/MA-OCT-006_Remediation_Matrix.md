**Write-cost convention (spec §9.1).** An `UPDATE` costs 1 row plus 1 for each index whose key changes. An `INSERT` or `DELETE` costs 1 row plus 1 for each index that holds the row. The index list comes from the day-1 D0 catalog (Query Ledger, Mon 28 Sep):

- `entity_master` has 9 full indexes: `idx_entity_master_direct_parent`, `_jurisdiction`, `_lei`, `_match_source`, `_normalized`, `_status`, `_type`, `_ultimate_parent`, and the `UNIQUE(normalized_name, type)` autoindex. It also has the partial index `idx_entity_master_phase3` (`lei, type, lei_status, direct_parent_lei, ultimate_parent_lei, direct_parent_exception`, `WHERE lei IS NOT NULL`).
- `entity_enrichment_queue` has its PK plus `idx_enrich_queue_status(status, retry_after)`.
- `entity_relationships` has its PK autoindex plus `idx_entity_rel_child` and `idx_entity_rel_parent`.

**Every write-cost figure below is labelled "006 est." It is a 006 estimate. Final costing belongs to the MA-OCT-011 and MA-OCT-013 specs.** The cost model and its row counts are in `WORK/day6_costs.py` / `day6_costs2.py`, with outputs `WORK/day6/cost_counts.json` and `cost_model.json`.

**Ranking rule (spec §9.1, with the D5-6 condition).** Rows are ranked by recoverable field-gaps × confidence, where high = 1.0. The ranking figure uses high-confidence counts only. Medium-confidence counts appear in their own column and are not ranked. Ties, including every row whose ranking figure is 0, break on lower write cost (one-time plus 4 weeks of recurring writes), then on lower risk. The (a) and (b) rows are listed after the ranked rows and rank on product impact, not on a recoverable count.
