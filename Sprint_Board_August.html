Working hypothesis (unconfirmed): the trigger was registered 2026-08-01T09:14:38Z and fired at the very next wall-clock 04:00 regardless of day-of-week — consistent with a known class of scheduler bug where the first run after a new registration is computed by nearest time-of-day rather than fully re-validating day-of-week, self-correcting from the second occurrence onward.
